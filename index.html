04 / Incident handling

How I troubleshoot

A repeatable, user-focused approach to support incidents — from first report to resolution or escalation.

STEP 01

Understand

Clarify the issue, affected user, scope, impact and recent changes.

STEP 02

Inspect

Check physical connection, interface state, IP address, gateway and DNS.

STEP 03

Test

Use approved checks such as ping, name resolution and service reachability.

STEP 04

Resolve / escalate

Verify the fix, document evidence and escalate with clear notes if needed.

This is a learning framework, not a claim of previous employment in a formal L1/L2 or NOC role.
